The ingredients

Five things we pick by hand.

No concentrates, no flavourings. What is on the label is what grew in the forest that summer.

  1. Sea buckthorn

    Hippophae rhamnoides · Late August

    Grows where the pines meet the dunes. Picked in late August, sour enough to make you blink.

  2. Wildflower honey

    Gauja meadows · Mid July

    Clover, yarrow, linden. Just enough to round off the sourness, never enough to make it sweet.

  3. Blackcurrant leaf

    Ribes nigrum, folium · Early June

    The leaf, not the berry. Green and peppery in June, steeped cold for nine hours.

  4. Juniper berry

    Juniperus communis · October

    From the raised bog, where juniper grows low and slow. Resin, pine, a little citrus.

  5. Wild rose hip

    Rosa rugosa · September

    Runs wild along the coast. Brings a soft, tea-like sweetness to sharp rhubarb.
