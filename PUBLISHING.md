## Project settings
- **Title:** Meža — A Digital Experience Born from the Forest
- **Cover:** `00-cover-1616x1264.jpg` (808×632 @2×), no crop needed
- **Description:**
  Meža is a concept botanical soda from the Baltic forest, with four recipes foraged around one spring in Sigulda, Latvia. The website turns the range into a walk through that forest. One real-time 3D bottle turns as you scroll and opens its label. It leads you through four flavour worlds and ends in a working shop. Concept, art direction, UI/UX, 3D and motion by Marta Jakovleva.
